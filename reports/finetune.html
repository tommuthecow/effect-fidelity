Accuracy per dollar: does a cheap fine-tune fix small simulators?

Builds on Kolluri, Wu, Park & Bernstein (2025), arXiv 2509.05830, who fine-tuned open models on SocSci210. Here: a deliberately tiny CPU LoRA (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, 480 SocSci210 answers, 1 epoch, r=8), with every study that might overlap our 17 experiments removed, evaluated on those 17 experiments, scored on effects and decisions, and priced.
